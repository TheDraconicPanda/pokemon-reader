Reader Emulator E1 uses melonDS 1.1, pinned by ios/core.lock.json.
melonDS: Copyright 2016-2025 melonDS team, GPL-3.0-or-later.
Upstream source and notices: https://github.com/melonDS-emu/melonDS

melonDS includes other components with their own notices and compatible terms,
including FreeBIOS, Teakra (MIT), fatfs (FatFs license), tiny-AES-c (Unlicense),
SHA-1 code, xxHash (BSD-2-Clause) and blip-buf (LGPL-2.1-or-later).
Their full source, copyright and license notices are retained unmodified in the
pinned upstream source tree and are available with that revision. The app's
Source.txt identifies the exact revisions for each distributed binary.

New code under ios/ is GPL-3.0-or-later. The browser Reader's existing license
metadata is not changed by this separate native prototype.

No Nintendo ROM, personal game save, proprietary BIOS or firmware dump is
included. FreeBIOS and generated DS firmware are supplied by the pinned core.
Networking, DSi emulation, microphone, accessories and GBA emulation are outside
this E1 frontend's supported scope. The source contains explicit no-op platform
implementations for these interfaces; the candidate has not passed iPhone game
compatibility or performance acceptance.
