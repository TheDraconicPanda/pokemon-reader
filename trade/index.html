Trade rules and coverage

This page follows local Generation IV party trading. Normal trade evolutions happen on receipt; evolution items must already be held and are consumed. Everstone blocks evolution except Kadabra. Friendship resets to 70 for non-Eggs. Eggs retain hatch progress and record the receiving console date; they do not register in the Pokédex yet.

Spiky-eared Pichu cannot enter the communication room. Remove Griseous Orbs and restore party Rotom, Giratina and Shaymin to base forms in-game before loading. Other supported forms and attached Mail travel with their Pokémon; Ball Capsules are detached. This tool does not create items or check how you originally obtained Pokémon.

Pokémon and Pokédex changes are simulated; communication logs, trainer-card trade counters and event unlocks are not reproduced. Rules, evidence and validation.
