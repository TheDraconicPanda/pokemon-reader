About the Johto guide

Floors and related rooms are grouped under their main location. Ledges, HMs and story progress can restrict travel. Non-Safari encounters, maps and location details have been audited against SoulSilver references; source links are available at each location. Full Kanto coverage remains planned.

Encounter percentages are species shares within the listed method, time and area after an encounter starts. Radio entries require the matching broadcast after the National Pokédex; outbreak entries require the announced outbreak and replace normal slots. Headbutt groups depend on the tree and Trainer ID; special trees require Rock Climb.

Safari’s mini Atlas has manual six-area navigation and the existing encounter reference. Safari layout/progression is not decoded from saves; its encounter/block mechanics audit and calculator remain separate work.

EV training finder

Choose a stat to find places to train in SoulSilver. Surfing spots come first. “100% useful” means every possible Pokémon in that encounter table awards the chosen EV; it does not mean a guaranteed encounter every step.

How to use these recommendations

Defeat the listed Pokémon to earn their base EVs. In mixed tables, flee from other encounters; a Pokémon that also awards another stat is labelled. Averages count the chosen stat over all encounters, assuming you defeat the useful Pokémon.

These are ordinary tables with the radio off and no outbreak, Repel or encounter-altering lead ability. Special tree groups and Unown unlocks are labelled. Fishing and Rock Smash percentages are conditional on a Pokémon appearing. Roamers and one-time battles are excluded. Check the location notes for access and story requirements.

Base yields use SoulSilver’s Generation IV data. Held-item and Pokérus bonuses, EV caps, encounter speed and battle time are not included. Safari Zone is outside this finder.
