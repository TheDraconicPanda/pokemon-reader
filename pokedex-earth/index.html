How to use these recommendations

Defeat the listed Pokémon to earn their base EVs. In mixed tables, flee from other encounters; a Pokémon that also awards another stat is labelled. Averages count the chosen stat over all encounters, assuming you defeat the useful Pokémon.

These are ordinary tables with the radio off and no outbreak, Repel or encounter-altering lead ability. Special tree groups and Unown unlocks are labelled. Fishing and Rock Smash percentages are conditional on a Pokémon appearing. Roamers and one-time battles are excluded. Check the location notes for access and story requirements.

Base yields use SoulSilver’s Generation IV data. Held-item and Pokérus bonuses, EV caps, encounter speed and battle time are not included. Safari Zone is outside this finder.
