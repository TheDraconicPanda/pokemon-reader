E3 adds three manual slots per ROM, three rotating automatic checkpoints,
compressed atomic storage, compatible-state validation, resume/game-save choices
and pre-restore cartridge backups. The two-minute checkpoint interval is a test
default, with five-minute and pause-only choices. Keep saving normally in-game.

The binary remains on GitHub; this is its download pointer. Signing, installation,
actual gameplay/recovery/lifecycle behavior, offload retention, stutter and matched
Delta battery/performance checks remain deferred. Abrupt termination can lose
changes since the last completed checkpoint. No zero-loss or energy claim is made.
