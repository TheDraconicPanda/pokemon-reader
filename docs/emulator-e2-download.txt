E2 implements an offline ROM library, durable cartridge saves with backup,
checked import/export and touch/controller/keyboard layouts. Save normally
inside the game. Abrupt termination can lose changes since the last disk commit.
Manual/automatic recovery states are E3 and are not included yet.

The binary remains on GitHub; this file is its download pointer. Signing,
installation, real-game save round trips, controls, lifecycle behavior and
Delta performance/battery comparisons on the iPhone remain untested. E1/E2
acceptance remains open. Phone testing is deferred; no efficiency win is claimed.
