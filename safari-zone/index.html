        Safari mechanics, sources and save support

        Each area holds at most 30 objects, including decorations. Objects add category points; stored active-area days increase their multipliers. A species may have several valid setups. Rearranging retains area age; replacing an area clears its objects.

        Baoba enables Area Customizer for his Sandshrew challenge. Object placement follows the completed challenge, National Dex and received object calls. Waiting days in plans are credited active-area days; clock penalties can delay them.

        Area arrangement, object types, stored area days, object unlock level and Baoba stage are decoded where independently verified. Entry access, Surf/rod access, clock penalty and time of day require manual configuration. Linked Safari setups require manual fallback. Stored days are not increased using this device’s date.
